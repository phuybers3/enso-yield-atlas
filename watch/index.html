Release notes and data corrections

Built from the ENSO yield database (1.79 million observations, 14,750 reporting units with daily weather), the working paper Agriculture and the 2026–27 El Niño, and the season tracker. We estimate historical relationships over 1981–2025 and apply ENSO and observed-weather scenarios. These conditional estimates carry uncertainty and do not measure total harvests or food supply.
