ENSO crop atlas

The atlas shows what the El Niño now under way implies for the coming harvests of maize, rice, wheat, soybean, sorghum and cassava. A world map colours each country by the expected departure of its 2026–27 harvest from trend under the central outlook of the Climate Prediction Center; a country page shows the same for its states or districts; and a page for each reporting place shows the record behind the number, with the season so far in temperature, rainfall and greenness. The site shows release 2026-10-19, with weather observations to 10 October 2026 and the index to September 2026. The release is in review: please do not cite or redistribute its numbers yet.
