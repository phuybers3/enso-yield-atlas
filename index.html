ENSO crop atlas

Explore how El Niño is associated with harvests of maize, rice, wheat, soybean, sorghum and cassava. Compare local historical relationships, the conditional 2026–27 outlook and the season so far in temperature, rainfall and greenness. Select a country or reporting unit to see the records behind its estimate.

Release 2026-11-02-inclusive displays each local ENSO response at its full random-effects weight toward its state and marks conditional skill at the level of weather-coherent growing regions. It uses matched temperature and rainfall observations through 25 October and NDVI through 28 October where coverage permits. Monthly ENSO observations end in September; the CPC outlook was issued on 8 October. Effect intervals remain under review and exclude unexplained harvest variability.
