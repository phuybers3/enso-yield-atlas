ENSO crop atlas

The atlas shows what the El Niño now under way implies for the coming harvests of maize, rice, wheat, soybean, sorghum and cassava. A world map colours each country by the expected departure of its 2026–27 harvest from trend under the central outlook of the Climate Prediction Center; a country page shows the same for its states or districts; and a page for each reporting place shows the record behind the number, with the season so far in temperature, rainfall and greenness. The site shows release 2026-10-19, with weather observations to 10 October 2026 and the index to September 2026. The release is in review: please do not cite or redistribute its numbers yet.

Earlier work

These applications are archived with the data they were built on. Their methods and counts describe those earlier analyses, not release 2026-10-19.

  • El Niño Crop WatchIssue 2026-10-06, published 7 October 2026: three screens per country, crop and season on what usually happens in an El Niño of the forecast strength, what has happened this season so far, and what it implies for the harvest.
  • Global yield atlasThe merged-database atlas of 26 September 2026: average yield, trend, ENSO response, record length and reporting resolution for 193 countries and territories, with source, period, calendar window, index and model controls.
  • Climate and crop atlasThe combined atlas: mean and 95th-percentile temperature and precipitation, their trends and ENSO sensitivity beside the crop responses.
  • ENSO responses by administrative unitThe explorer of 24 September 2026: 8,738 fitted series across 288 panels of HarvestStat Asia, with +3 °C event scenarios and downloadable estimates.
